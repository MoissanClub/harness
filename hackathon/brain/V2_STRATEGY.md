## 1. What went wrong and why

Four root causes, none of them a wrong constant.

**The hand is placed by a model that is wrong by 10 cm, and the corrections check the model against itself.** g1-fetch puts the palm 0.15 m ahead of the wrist (`geom.py` `EE_OFFSET_X = 0.15`, "MEASURE in g1-fetch"); the jog found the real palm 9.6-9.7 cm further (`2026-10-19-office-grab.md` line 31, `jog_today.log` line 19). `brain.py` (HEAD) hides this behind `--dx -0.096` (line 21) and "corrects" with `hand_meas()` = FK of the measured joints (line 257-258, 265-270), so the pre-grasp lands within 1-8 mm of a point that is itself 10 cm off, and the offset changes with pose: the jog stalled at palm x 0.41 while commanding further (`jog_today.log` lines 21-23). The one-frame hand-and-can measurement from Oct 19 (`grab_lateral.py`) was dropped.

**The slide throws the correction away and the arm sags.** Line 402 `cur = pre.copy()` restarts from the nominal pre-grasp and line 406 re-solves with no measured correction. Measured z after the slide: 0.101 for a pre-grasp at 0.133 (`live6.log` 59, 62), 0.100 for 0.136 (`live7.log` 59, 62), 0.121 for 0.144 (`live4.log` 57). That is 2.1-3.6 cm low, growing with reach, which is why five of seven autonomous Tuesday misses read pinky 0.9 with three fingers stalled at 0.46-0.56 (`live1.log` 95, `live2.log` 58, `live3.log` 78, `live7.log` 84): the hand met the can's base. **Guards replaced feasibility.** The lane (`brain.py` 36-37, y 0.06-0.30, x 0.31-0.50) is two incidents: the Oct 19 block at y 0.008 with the forearm forced to +x (`XDIR_` line 10; office-grab line 40) and the jog's reach stall. Six of fourteen Tuesday orders were lane refusals, including the dry-run can at `[0.401, 0.03, 0.14]` (`dry1.log` 50, 56). The kinematics review shows that pose is reachable with the forearm yawed 20-25 deg inward.

**Stops and verdicts came from single incidents.** Contact is a fixed 0.015 rad over a baseline read once (lines 401, 409) that itself ranges 0.044-0.069 rad; it fired after one step 6.6 cm from the can (`live7.log` 62) and stayed False with the fingers on the can (`live1.log` 73). The strict verify rule (line 424-426) rejected `[0.09, 0.67, 0.49, 0.54, 0.49, 0.9]`, a real hold (`live1.log` 75), and the blind retry pushed the can.

**Process and the session question.** The brain session ran from 2026-10-20T01:40Z to 2026-10-21T00:54Z (get_session), one session that designed, built, deployed, drove and reviewed; `DEV_WORKFLOW.md` (its own untracked note) says "one 1000-message session designed, built, deployed and drove". Was context the cause? Partly: the Monday evidence above was in the transcript and was not used on Tuesday, and three variables changed at once at 024115 (`--pause 8 --dx -0.06 --verify majority`, n = 2) with two commits five minutes apart (9fc447a, 83d7c0e, the latter a release rule never run on a guest). Yes, start a new session, and one per phase from now on. ## 2. Rules from now on

1. **No geometric literal in motion code.** Every distance lives in `hackathon/brain/calib.json` with value, unit, frame, method, date, pose, can, n, log path; `brain.py` prints the table at start, logs its hash into every order, logs each CLI override as a deviation, and refuses stale entries. Runtime quantities (table plane, can size, palm-to-can offset, slide z, tracking baseline) are measured per order and logged, never stored.
2. **Hardware constants come from the URDF, not from a session.** Link lengths, joint limits, the camera pose and the torso box are read from `g1_body23.urdf`; the only measured hardware constant is the real palm point (0.246 m along the wrist roll axis).
3. **Every loop is closed on a sensor.** The pre-grasp converges on the head camera's hand-to-can offset; the slide re-applies the measured joint correction each step; the grasp is verified by the can rising in the head frame. Stops derived from tracking error are safety aborts only.
4. **One change, N trials, per-stage table.** Hypothesis and prediction written before the run; one variable between runs; at least five trials per configuration over three can positions; results reported as located / feasible / pre-grasp error / slide-end offset / stop reason / hand-state pattern / lift / release, with n. A streak across configurations is reported per configuration.
5. **Session hygiene.** One session per phase: brief, offline build with replay tests, robot block (an experiment list, no redesign, no code edits on PC2), post-mortem. After three misses the block stops and the logs are read. A rule that moves the hand or releases a can is not committed without a logged on-robot trial or a replay test against recorded hand states.

## 3. Reach: feasibility replaces the lane

Chain from `g1_body23.urdf` (lines 635-734): shoulder pitch at pelvis (0, 0.100, 0.292), upper arm 0.193 m, elbow to wrist 0.101 m, limits shoulder roll -1.588..+2.2515 rad, yaw +/-2.618, elbow -1.047..+2.094, wrist roll +/-1.972. Real palm 0.246 m along the roll axis (0.15 model + 0.096 jog); the offset then rotates with the forearm instead of being a +x constant that is 1-2 cm wrong in z at the lane ends. Torso from the URDF meshes: x -0.06..+0.07, y +/-0.098 at z 0.10-0.16, chest +/-0.108 at z 0.26.

One search variable, the forearm yaw psi toward the midline, -30..+60 deg in 5 deg steps, visited outward from psi_pref = clamp(10 + (0.09 - y) * 100, 0, 50) deg. For each psi: forearm d = (cos psi, -sin psi, 0), palm normal n = d x z (the only thumb-up family; the mirror needs a 180 deg roll), grasp palm P = C - (r + 0.01) n, pre-grasp P - 0.03 n, high waypoints +0.12 z. Solve the 4-joint IK with the yaw held hard (not `dir_weight 0.5`), seeded from the previous psi; wrist roll from `wrist_roll_for_palm(n)`. Forearm pitch is an outcome, read per psi.

Accept when all of these hold at grasp, pre-grasp, every 1 cm slide pose and both waypoints: IK error <= 5 mm (not the 35 mm of `--tol`), |pitch| <= 15 deg, lower upper arm >= 3 cm from the torso box (log-calibrated: grasps had 1.7-3.4 cm, the two 030059 misses 0.7-1.0, the Oct 19 block -4.3), forearm >= 4 cm, hand >= 6 cm, right-arm capsule >= 5 cm, hand bottom >= 1 cm above the table, every joint >= 10 deg from its limit. Choose the smallest |pitch|, ties to smaller |psi|. Refuse only when nothing passes, naming the constraint.

Numbers: a can at (0.35, 0.00, 0.13) at psi 30 gives IK error 0, pitch +1.4 deg, elbow at (0.069, 0.205, 0.130), clearances 6.3 / 9.7 / 11.0 cm, q = (-16, +22, -29, +2 deg); at psi 0 the elbow sits 5.1 cm inside the torso. The refused `[0.401, 0.03, 0.14]` passes at psi 20-25. On a 3 cm grid at z 0.13 the region is 202 cells against the lane's 54: the centre line to x 0.48 and 12 cm right of centre at x <= 0.36. The far bound is physical (elbow ring radius 0.105 m, level palm reach 0.24-0.45 m; x 0.50 needs 19 deg droop), so prefer cans 0.26-0.42 m out.

The uncommitted `geom.py` is close but must change: `TORSO_BOX` y +/-0.12 is a guess (its own comment says re-measure), `clearance()` skips the upper arm, which is where the Oct 19 collision was, `plan_reach` takes the first feasible of yaws 0,10,20,30,45 with tolerance 0.035 instead of the best of a full sweep at 5 mm, and the palm is still 0.15 m.

## 5. Grasp: strategy and the visual servo loop

Closure pose, from the 12 holds: can axis at the palm centre, palm face r + 1 cm from the axis along -n, palm centre 1.5 cm below mid-height (trial 8 at -2 cm was the deepest wrap, office-grab line 28). Grasp z = measured table plane + h/2 - 0.015, never the box-centre depth.

Pre-grasp: raise, high, lower to P - 0.03 n, thumb opposed. Then the loop, in one aligned head frame (flush 3): can = YOLOE box and mask, median depth of the central half, axis +r along the ray; hand = depth points above table + 2 cm in a +/-15 cm window around the FK-predicted palm, minus the dilated can mask, largest cluster, features fingertip extent along d, nearest point along n, median z (`geom.hand_blob` does most of this). Setpoint K = these features' offset from the can axis at one owner-approved centred pose, stored once as a hand constant. Error e = (axis + K) - features in (d, n, z). Command cmd += clip(e_d, e_z, +/-3 cm) through the IK with the chosen psi, settle 0.6 s, keep the proprioceptive hold on the new command, re-measure. Converged at |e_d|, |e_z| < 1 cm on two frames; budget 3 iterations (about 1.2 s each, the 4 s pause it replaces). Cross-check once with the Gemma box "the robot's white hand"; disagreement > 3 cm aborts. If the cluster scatters > 1.5 cm, a 5 cm ArUco tag on the glove replaces it.

Approach: two half-steps along +n sized from e_n, re-measure between; abort if the axis moved > 1.5 cm (pushed). Close when the gap < 1 cm; no commanded press. Tracking error > 2x the per-pose baseline and lag > 1.2 cm are aborts only. Log q, measured FK and tracking error per step.

Verdict: thumb < 0.3 and at least 3 of 4 fingers in (0.25, 0.70); hand state logged after each stage. One mapped correction, then assist: thumb 0.9 with fingers stalled (can forward of the palm): open, +3 cm along d, re-close. Pinky 0.9 alone (hand low): open, +2 cm z, re-close. All 0.9: YOLOE re-detect; moved > 2 cm means pushed, re-locate; else -3 cm along d. Lift 6 cm and verify in the head frame: the can's point must rise by lift +/- 2 cm (YOLOE points repeat within 7 mm, `live4.log`), otherwise it is a miss whatever the stall says. Offer inside the head camera's view (47.6 deg down, half-FOV 27.4 x 21.3 deg); release on a new depth cluster within 6 cm of the can for two frames confirmed by one yes/no, a tug > 0.03 on a stalled finger with the setpoint re-sent, or a let-go transcript never within 4 s of TTS.
