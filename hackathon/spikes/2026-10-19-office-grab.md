## Grab attempt 1 (`grab_tabletop.py --tol 0.035`): failed cleanly
Pre-grasp 10 cm behind the can, forward approach at 6 cm/s with the can beside the palm, staged grasp. Fingers closed to 0.90 = the command, so nothing was in the hand; the skill opened and aborted, arms released, service restored. Owner's observations: the table edge blocked the fingers, and the fingers never wrapped the can. Re-detection afterwards: the can had been pushed from 44 cm to 63 cm ahead. Diagnosis: `grab_can`'s forward approach (designed for a fridge shelf) drags the fingers across the table edge and does not bring the can into the hand's "C"; plus open-loop error (IK 2.8 cm, 0.07 rad gravity sag, unmeasured `ee_offset_x` and `palm_inward_wrist_roll`).

## Side-stepping and the perception-closed approach loop (owner's request; robot in the stand, FSM 500)
- `sidestep.py` (open-loop lateral pulses through g1-fetch `UnitreeLoco.set_velocity`, 0.2 m/s): 0.6 s gave 2.2 cm, 1.5 s gave 27 cm, 1.2 s back gave 13 cm: the controller's ramp makes the displacement very nonlinear in pulse length, and each pulse adds a few cm of drift forward/back and about 1 deg of yaw.
- `sidestep_to.py` (odometry-closed: 0.7 s pulses, re-measure, stop within 3 cm): reached 10.2 cm of a 12 cm target in 3 pulses, with one 12.5 cm overshoot pulse corrected.
- `approach_can.py` (perception-closed: YOLOE boxes, colour verified by asking Gemma about a crop of each can, a can touching the image edge triggers a step toward it, lateral then forward adjustments until the target is in the lane 10-25 cm left / 32-40 cm ahead): colour verification worked (crops answered white/silver for the white can), but 8 iterations did not converge: asked steps of 8 cm produced 5.9, 4.9, -2.4, 8.4, 11.1, 1.2 and 12.2 cm, and an asked forward step of 10 cm produced a 9 cm lateral move instead; the can kept hugging the left image edge and the robot drifted about 45 cm left and 15 cm back. Owner stopped the test. Likely causes: the stand's straps and the controller's ramp distort short pulses, and the step controller maps body-frame requests through odometry while the robot yaws. Stepping needs a free-standing robot and a proper velocity controller (ramped command held until the odometry target is met) before it can be part of the demo; for the stand-based demo, place the cans inside the hand's lane.

Post-mortem of the last approach run (owner stopped it as the robot neared the table): iteration 7 finally saw the can fully, 25 cm ahead, nearer than the lane's 32 cm minimum, and the loop commanded a 10 cm step FORWARD. The correction was computed as "aim minus can position" and applied to the robot, which is the sign of the can's desired motion, not the robot's; the lateral correction had the same inversion. Fixed in `scripts/approach_can.py`: signs inverted, no forward step while the can is nearer than the lane minimum or the depth image shows anything within 0.30 m ahead, a 0.30 m cap on total displacement, a stop when no safe step exists. Stepping remains out of the demo until the robot is free-standing with a ramped velocity controller.
