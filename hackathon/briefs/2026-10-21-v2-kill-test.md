# Run plan: v2-kill-test (the next robot session)

Written by the v2 build session (S0 draft, revised after `hackathon/brain/V2_REVIEW.md` rounds 1 and 2); the owner approves it before
the robot is switched on. Two experiments of V2_STRATEGY, no grasps: the swivel-and-slide air test (experiment 3) as the first motion
of the session, because v2 moves the arm on a new planner and a new motion layer, then the hand measured in the head camera at the
pre-grasp (experiment 2, the kill test). Every brain command carries `--park home --expect-calib 9a8165bf6976c20f` and, when the arm
may move, `--table-edge E` (the owner's tape; without it the arm is not enabled).

## Question

Does the head camera measure the hand's features (fingertip front, nearest face, height) against the can axis with a scatter under
1 cm, and does one commanded correction leave a residual under 1 cm, at three can positions the camera covers?

## The deciding number

- Measured quantity, unit, frame: `off` = features - can axis along (forearm d, palm normal n, z), metres, pelvis frame, from the
  `probe:` line (median of 5 frames) and `scatter` (std over the 5 frames, printed on the same line and in `orders.jsonl` under `probe`).
- Tolerance: scatter < 0.010 m on each axis; after `--probe-servo` with K and a 2.5 cm pre-grasp offset, the residual |e_d|, |e_z|
  < 0.010 m.
- Pass criterion with n per class: at the three kill-test slots (x 0.38, y 0.12 / 0.09 / 0.06; one can, the same can throughout):
  scatter under 1 cm at 3 of 3; the hand in view at 3 of 3 (the `probe:` line shows `margins image A px window B px` with A and B at
  least 8, and no "AT THE IMAGE EDGE" / "AT THE WINDOW EDGE"); in the servo block, the residual under 1 cm within two iterations at
  2 of 2 slots. `off` minus `model_off` is recorded at each slot, not judged: the slots differ in y only (V2_STRATEGY's 2-4 cm change
  was stated for slots spread along x; the mock gives 1.1 cm here).
- Edge probe, reported separately and outside the pass criterion: slot y 0.17. With the URDF camera mount the hand at that pre-grasp
  touches the left image border; expect "AT THE IMAGE EDGE".
- Fail means: the blob is not a usable hand feature (scatter >= 1 cm, or the hand not seen at a slot the camera covers): the next plan
  measures with an ArUco tag on the glove before anything else is built (V2_STRATEGY section 5); no servo-based grasp on this code.

## Stop rules for this plan

The general rules in `DEV_WORKFLOW.md` section 4 apply. Plan-specific:

- `CODE 8e226e9` missing, `(dirty)` on the calib line, or `CALIB IDENTITY FAILED`: stop, no motion.
- The dry run's target disagrees with the tape by more than 3 cm, or the plane by more than 2 cm: stop, the camera transform or the
  table fit is wrong; no motion. An air target's table check refuses by itself (no table seen under it, or more than 2 cm from T).
- The held hand at 20 or 30 cm is not refused, or `valid depth` under 80% with nothing in front: stop; the 0.30 m rule cannot see
  that zone on this robot.
- A `... path:` line refused by the keep-out (the hand below the table top + 3 cm within 2 cm of E): the brain refuses by itself; a
  refusal is a stop for this plan (the edge or the raise is not what the plan assumed).
- Air test: any contact of the elbow, forearm or hand with the torso, the stand, its straps or the table: L2+B, stop; no more motion
  on this plan (`--clear` only after the gap is measured and written).
- Air test: a `SKILL FAILED` message (the brain refuses a corrected `pre:` err over 0.02 m and a sag over 0.07 m by itself), or
  `My arm stopped and is holding`: stop; the model disagrees with the arm, the kill test is not run. The `up:` and `high:` lines are
  uncorrected: their err is the sag baseline, recorded (Tuesday logged 1.9-2.6 / 2.5-4.7 cm).
- Kill test: "hand not seen", "AT THE IMAGE EDGE" or "AT THE WINDOW EDGE" at two of the three covered slots, N under 300 points, or the
  blob bbox not on the hand in `measure_probe.jpg`: stop and read the frames; the ArUco plan follows.
- Kill test: `off` minus `model_off` larger than 8 cm on any axis, or `gemma hand box on the blob` under 50% at two slots: stop, the
  camera-to-pelvis transform, the blob or the palm model is wrong.
- Dry run: "I cannot see the table" or "at the edge of my view" at a slot: read `bundle_locate_depth.png` and move the slot before any
  motion (no box-depth fallback exists in v2).
- **Stuck** (the robot says "My arm stopped and is holding. Press h to see the way out."; a refused or stopped move): the arm holds its
  last target and nothing moves without the operator. The reminder repeats every 30 s. The procedure: (1) L2+B ready; (2) `h` + Enter:
  the brain plans the way out from where the arm is and says it, nothing moves ("The way out: retreat back 17 centimetres, then bridge
  0 centimetres, then home 40 centimetres. Press y to run it."); (3) if the owner agrees with what they see, `y` + Enter runs it,
  announced (a part already done is not repeated: the arm never walks further out); `a`, `q` or Ctrl-C during it stops it again;
  anything else keeps holding; (4) "I have no safe way out", or a way out the owner does not like: hold the arm, L2+B (damping), then
  type `quit!` (the brain exits without releasing). Never `quit!` without L2+B. `h` is accepted only while stuck.
- `q` or Ctrl-C with the hand out is not a stop: the brain brings the arm home first ("Moving my arm back before I stop.", about 25 s at
  half speed) and releases it only at home; a second `q`, `a` or Ctrl-C stops the arm there (the stuck procedure). The only stop is L2+B.
- `p` only keeps the next order from starting; the arm stays held at home (it does not pause the streamer).
- Two identical faults, or the owner's "stop": the block ends.

## Air-test pass criterion (experiment 3)

At 3 of 3 targets: the sag baseline lines at the raise and at the high waypoint present (recorded, no lower bound; over 0.07 m the
brain stops by itself); the corrected `pre:` err under 0.006 m; five `airN: moved` lines between 0.007 and 0.012 m; the `slide end:`
error under 0.010 m; the owner's elbow-to-torso and elbow-to-stand gaps `clear` (`o` lines); the retreat and the retraced descent
completed without contact and without a stuck. Fail at any target: no kill test on this plan.

## Risks

- The motion layer has never moved the real arm. The air test is the first motion: half speed (`--speed 0.5`: every Cartesian speed,
  the approach steps and the joint step per target halved), hand open, no can, every path solved before it starts, a 2 cm per-target
  palm cap and a 0.01 rad per-target joint cap at half speed, IK within 5 mm, 5 degree joint-limit margins, the keep-out against E.
- The return home retraces the recorded raise from wherever a stop left the arm; a joint-space move only from below the table-top height
  behind the raise waypoint. Anywhere else the arm holds (stuck) and the operator decides.
- The 0.30 m rule refuses when less than 70% of the depth pixels are valid; something small inside the D435i's minimum range may not lower
  it that much (the no-motion check with a hand at 20 and 30 cm decides).
- The hand blob's search (a 200 x 170 px window, 25 cm radius, grown once when touched) can take in an object the detector does not see;
  only detected cans are excluded.
- Grasps (not in this plan): the hand closes only on a visual gap that agrees with the model within 1 cm; a can-axis bias of 1-1.5 cm
  (cans at y >= 0.17 and right of centre, the axis-aligned box of a slanted can) makes those approaches abort with no close (safe). The
  high waypoint (12 cm above the pre-grasp, about 5 cm lower with the sag) leaves the hand below the top of a 13.5 cm can: a transit path
  that crosses another can sweeps through it (the mock's three-can scene); the kill test's path stays 7-8 cm beside its one can.
- `V2_REVIEW_findings.json` holds 113 round-1 findings not yet triaged; `--palm-real` does not reach the kinematics (bound at import)
  and must not be overridden on the command line.
