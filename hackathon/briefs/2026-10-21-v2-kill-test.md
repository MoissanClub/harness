# Run plan: v2-kill-test (the next robot session)

Written by the v2 build session (S0 draft, revised after `hackathon/brain/V2_REVIEW.md` rounds 1-4); the owner approves it before the
robot is switched on. Two experiments of V2_STRATEGY, no grasps: the swivel-and-slide air test (experiment 3) as the first motion of the
session, because v2 moves the arm on a new planner and a new motion layer, then the hand measured in the head camera at the pre-grasp
(experiment 2, the kill test). One brain process per run (`--orders 1`): it parks at the start, runs one order and exits with the arm at
home, released ("Releasing my arm."); the can is moved only between processes. Every command carries `--orders 1 --park home --no-grasp
--expect-calib ae553548d47b677a --table-z T`, and every command after the first no-motion run also `--table-edge E --fingertip F
--palm-half-height H --blind-max BM` (the owner's tape and the measured blind region, below; without E or T the arm is not enabled).
The agent types the commands, the keys and the `o` lines; the owner speaks the judgements and keeps L2+B.

## Question

Does the head camera measure the hand's features (fingertip front, nearest face, height) against the can axis with a scatter under
1 cm, and does one commanded correction leave a residual under 1 cm, at three can positions the camera covers?

## The deciding number

- Measured quantity, unit, frame: `off` = features - can axis along (forearm d, palm normal n, z), metres, pelvis frame, from the
  `probe:` line (median of 5 frames) and `scatter` (std over the 5 frames, printed on the same line and in `orders.jsonl` under `probe`).
- Tolerance: scatter < 0.010 m on each axis; after `--probe-servo` with K and a 2.5 cm pre-grasp offset, the residual |e_d|, |e_z|
  < 0.010 m.
- Pass criterion with n per class: at the three kill-test slots (x 0.38, y 0.12 / 0.09 / 0.06; the purple 12.2 cm can throughout):
  scatter under 1 cm at 3 of 3; the hand in view at 3 of 3 (the `probe:` line shows `margins image A px window B px` with A and B at
  least 8, none of "AT THE IMAGE EDGE", "AT THE WINDOW EDGE", "NEAR FACE INSIDE THE CAN", "gemma hand box: none", and no `probe FLAGGED`
  line); `measure_probe.jpg` shows the blob box on the hand with the fingertips inside it; in the servo block, the residual under 1 cm
  within two corrections at 2 of 2 slots. Scatter tests stability, not accuracy: the mock's `off` is not a prediction (a box hand with
  full depth returns).
- `off` minus `model_off` is printed at each slot (`probe: off - model_off [..]`) and judged only against wide expected ranges: d 0.03
  to 0.12, n 0.00 to 0.04, z 0.00 to 0.07 (the mock: 0.090-0.097, 0.020, 0.043-0.044). Outside them the brain flags the probe itself (`probe FLAGGED: the
  reading is outside the expected range: not used`, spoken "Not used.") and stores the reading as `off_flagged`, never as `off`.
- Edge probe, reported separately and outside the pass criterion: slot y 0.17. With the URDF camera mount the hand at that pre-grasp
  touches the left image border; expect "AT THE IMAGE EDGE" (then the robot says "My hand is at the edge of what I can measure. Not
  used." and no `off` is stored).
- Fail means: the blob is not a usable hand feature (scatter >= 1 cm, or the hand not seen at a slot the camera covers): the next plan
  measures with an ArUco tag on the glove before anything else is built (V2_STRATEGY section 5); no servo-based grasp on this code.

## Stop rules for this plan

The general rules in `DEV_WORKFLOW.md` section 4 apply. Plan-specific:

- `CODE 7869c62` missing, `(dirty)` on the calib line, or `CALIB IDENTITY FAILED` / `REFUSED: no --expect-calib`: stop, no motion.
- The dry run's target disagrees with the tape by more than 3 cm: stop, the camera transform or the table fit is wrong; no motion. A
  plane more than 2 cm from T refuses the order by itself (no motion), as does an air target's table check (no table seen under it, or
  more than 2 cm from T).
- The 0.30 m zone is pelvis x < 0.30 (about 17-22 cm in front of the chest), |y| < 0.12, z 0.15-0.50 above the pelvis (0.99-1.34 m above
  the floor); the head camera sees only up to about 0.36-0.46 of it. The rule also refuses one connected region of invalid depth larger
  than BM of the frame (the robot's own arm silhouette left out), in every state, before high, before the lowering, before every air,
  back and approach step and before every servo correction. The hand held at 10 or 15 cm is not refused, or the one at 30 cm is refused,
  or `valid depth` is under 80% with nothing in front: stop; the rule cannot see that zone on this robot. A largest blind region over
  0.25 with nothing in front: read `bundle_air_check_depth.png` before setting BM (a far surface can be covered or moved).
- E outside the window above: stop.
- Air test: any contact of the elbow, forearm or hand with the torso, the stand, its straps or the table: L2+B, then `quit!`; no more
  motion on this plan (`--clear` only after the gap is measured and written).
- Air test: a `SKILL FAILED` line (the brain refuses a corrected `pre:` err over 0.02 m, a sag over 0.07 m and a stalled slide or back
  step by itself; the arm then holds), any hold, or the fourth run's final-line lag L at 0.003 or more: stop; the model disagrees with
  the arm, the kill test is not run. The `up:` and `high:` lines are uncorrected: their err is the sag baseline, recorded (Tuesday logged
  1.9-2.6 / 2.5-4.7 cm).
- Kill test and servo block: any contact refusal (`SKILL FAILED: pre-grasp: ... (contact?)`, `servo N: ... (contact?)`, `the hand stopped
  following ... on the final line (contact?)`) or the hand seen on the table in `cam3_pregrasp.jpg` or `measure_probe.jpg`: the block
  ends after the hold procedure; compare run (4)'s plane with the tape before anything else. A taped H over 0.05: run with
  `--palm-half-height H` (the floor rises with it) and note it.
- Kill test: "hand not seen", "AT THE IMAGE EDGE", "AT THE WINDOW EDGE", "NEAR FACE INSIDE THE CAN", "gemma hand box: none" or `probe
  FLAGGED` at two of the three covered slots, N under 300 points, or the blob bbox not on the hand in `measure_probe.jpg`: stop and read
  the frames; the ArUco plan follows.
- Kill test: `off` minus `model_off` outside d 0.03-0.12, n 0.00-0.04, z 0.00-0.07, or the Gemma hand box on the blob under 50% at two
  slots: stop, the camera-to-pelvis transform, the blob or the palm model is wrong.
- Dry run: "I cannot see the table" or "at the edge of my view" at a slot: read `bundle_locate_depth.png` and move the slot before any
  motion (no box-depth fallback exists in v2).
- Something in the 0.30 m zone during a move: the arm holds where it is ("Something is too close in front of me. Please step back.");
  the person steps back; then the hold procedure.
- **Keys** (one meaning in every state): Enter starts the order the brain is waiting for (anywhere else it is ignored); `q`, `a` or
  Ctrl-C (also SIGTERM, SIGHUP) stop: the arm freezes at its next 20 ms target and holds (the hold procedure); `y` runs the move the
  brain has just announced and is waiting for (anywhere else it is ignored); `quit!` (or `quit`) exits without moving or releasing
  anything, only after L2+B; `o k=v ..` logs an observation; any other line prints the key list. L2+B on the remote is the emergency
  stop at any time. There is no pause key: the can is moved only between processes, with the arm released at home.
- **Hold** (after a stop key, a refusal of the 0.30 m rule, a head camera that cannot be read, a `SKILL FAILED` or an error with the arm
  out, an `ENABLE MISMATCH`, and at the end of every probe): the arm holds its last target and nothing moves without `y`. Step by step:
  1. L2+B ready.
  2. The brain plans the way home from where the arm is and says it, for example "The way home: retreat up 5 centimetres, then retreat
     back 16 centimetres, then turn the wrist 36 degrees, then home 42 centimetres. Press y to run it." (repeated every 30 s). The
     distances are straight lines between the palm's end points, not the path; "turn the wrist" is the bridge onto the recorded raise.
  3. `y` runs it, announced ("Up and back.", "Turning my wrist.", "Home."). With the arm home the process ends and releases it ("Releasing
     my arm."); after a stop key it ends even when orders are left.
  4. `q`, `a` or Ctrl-C during the way home freezes the arm there: back to step 2 with the new way home. `q` while the brain waits for
     `y`: it says the way home again; nothing moves.
  5. "I have no safe way home. Hold my arm, press L2 plus B, then type quit with an exclamation mark.", a way home the owner does not
     like, or anything unexpected: the owner holds the arm, presses L2+B (damping), then the agent types `quit!`: the brain exits without
     moving or releasing the arm. Never `quit!` without L2+B. Leaving damping while the brain still runs brings back the held target, so
     exit first. Then the owner lowers the arm by hand to the side; the next command's start refuses an arm not at the side.
  6. After a crash of the brain with the arm out (a traceback, no prompt): the owner holds the arm and presses L2+B, then lowers it by
     hand.
- Two identical faults, or the owner's "stop": the block ends.

## Air-test pass criterion (experiment 3)

At 4 of 4 runs: the sag baseline lines at the raise and at the high waypoint present (recorded, no lower bound; over 0.07 m the brain
stops by itself); the corrected `pre:` err under 0.006 m; the five `airN: moved` lines adding up to at least 0.045 m (the brain stops a
step under 0.005 m by itself); the `slide end:` error under 0.010 m; at the fourth run the final-line lag under 0.003 m; the owner's
elbow-to-torso and elbow-to-stand gaps as the model expects (above); the retreat and the retraced descent completed without contact and
without a hold. Fail at any run: no kill test on this plan.

## Risks

- The motion layer has never moved the real arm. The air test is the first motion: half speed (`--speed 0.5`: every Cartesian speed,
  the approach steps and the joint step per target halved), hand open, no can, every path solved before it starts, a 2 cm per-target
  palm cap and a 0.01 rad per-target joint cap at half speed, IK within 5 mm, 5 degree joint-limit margins (the raise waypoint leaves
  the elbow 6-11 degrees from its limit in the mock), the keep-out against E with the taped F.
- The raise goes up beside the hip first (2 cm behind it, to the table top + 3 cm), then to the raise waypoint. The return home
  retraces the recorded raise from wherever a stop left the arm; a joint-space move only from below the table-top height behind the raise
  waypoint; anywhere else the arm holds and the owner decides.
- The table floor uses the hand box (F, H, the assumed back extent 0.045 and half thickness 0.02) through the predicted orientation on
  arrival, on the higher of the fitted plane and T; the final line onto a goal near the floor stops when the hand falls more than 6 mm
  behind the command. With the plane and the tape both 15 mm low and the hand 2 cm lower than the model, the mock's push is 5 mm
  (`killtable`).
- The blind-region rule needs BM from this robot's own depth with nothing in front; a far surface with no depth over BM refuses every
  move (no motion, the session can then answer nothing until it is covered or BM is re-measured).
- The hand blob's search (a 200 x 170 px window, 25 cm radius, grown once when touched) can take in an object the detector does not see;
  only detected cans are excluded; the near-face check catches can points in the blob.
- Grasps are not in this plan (`--no-grasp` on every command): the transit height over other cans, the visual gap's dependence on K_n,
  the blind stall-pattern nudge and the can-axis bias are recorded in `hackathon/brain/HANDOFF.md` for the grasp session.
- `V2_REVIEW_findings.json` holds 113 round-1 findings not yet triaged; `--palm-real` does not reach the kinematics (bound at import)
