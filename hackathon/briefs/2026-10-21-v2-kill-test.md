# Run plan: v2-kill-test (the next robot session)

Written by the v2 build session (S0 draft, revised after `hackathon/brain/V2_REVIEW.md`); the owner approves it before the robot is
switched on. Two experiments of V2_STRATEGY, no grasps: the swivel-and-slide air test (experiment 3) as the first motion of the session,
because v2 moves the arm on a new planner and a new motion layer, then the hand measured in the head camera at the pre-grasp (experiment
2, the kill test). Every command runs with `--park home`.

## Question

Does the head camera measure the hand's features (fingertip front, nearest face, height) against the can axis with a scatter under
1 cm, and does one commanded correction leave a residual under 1 cm, at three can positions the camera covers?

## The deciding number

- Measured quantity, unit, frame: `off` = features - can axis along (forearm d, palm normal n, z), metres, pelvis frame, from the
  `probe:` line (median of 5 frames) and `scatter` (std over the 5 frames, in `orders.jsonl` under `probe`).
- Tolerance: scatter < 0.010 m on each axis; after `--probe-servo` with K, the residual |e_d|, |e_z| < 0.010 m.
- Pass criterion with n per class: at the three kill-test slots (x 0.38, y 0.12 / 0.09 / 0.06; one can, the same can throughout):
  scatter under 1 cm at 3 of 3; the hand in view at 3 of 3 (no "AT THE IMAGE EDGE" on the `probe:` line); the measured-minus-model
  offset (`off` minus `model_off`) changing by 2-4 cm across the slots; the servo residual under 1 cm within two iterations at 2 of 3.
- Edge probe, reported separately and outside the pass criterion: slot y 0.17. With the URDF camera mount the hand at that pre-grasp
  touches the left image border (mock render: margin 0 px, against 34 / 77 / 98 px at y 0.12 / 0.09 / 0.06); expect "AT THE IMAGE EDGE".
- Fail means: the blob is not a usable hand feature (scatter >= 1 cm, or the hand not seen at a slot the camera covers): the next plan
  measures with an ArUco tag on the glove before anything else is built (V2_STRATEGY section 5); no servo-based grasp on this code.

## Stop rules for this plan

The general rules in `DEV_WORKFLOW.md` section 4 apply. Plan-specific:

- The dry run's target disagrees with the tape by more than 3 cm, or the plane by more than 2 cm: stop, the camera transform or the
  table fit is wrong; no motion. The same for an air target's `table under the air target` line (the brain refuses it by itself).
- A raise or descent line `... path: below z Z the hand stays behind x X` with X within 2 cm of the taped table edge (before
  `table_edge_m` is in calib.json; afterwards the brain refuses such a path by itself): stop.
- Air test: any contact of the elbow, forearm or hand with the torso, the stand, its straps or the table: L2+B, stop; no more motion
  on this plan (`--clear` only after the gap is measured and written).
- Air test: the corrected `pre:` line with `err` over 0.02 m, a `SKILL FAILED` message, or `My arm stopped over the table`: stop; the
  model disagrees with the arm, the kill test is not run. The `up:` and `high:` lines are uncorrected: their err is the sag baseline
  (expected 0.02-0.05 m, recorded, not a stop rule; over 0.07 m is a stop).
- Kill test: "hand not seen" or "AT THE IMAGE EDGE" at two of the three covered slots, N under 300 points, or the blob bbox not on the
  hand in `measure_probe.jpg`: stop and read the frames; the ArUco plan follows.
- Kill test: `off` minus `model_off` larger than 8 cm on any axis, or `gemma hand vs blob` over 3 cm at two slots: stop, the
  camera-to-pelvis transform, the blob or the palm model is wrong.
- Dry run: "I cannot see the table" or "at the edge of my view" at a slot: read `bundle_locate_depth.png` and move the slot before any
  motion (no box-depth fallback exists in v2).
- Two identical faults, or the owner's "stop": the block ends.

## Air-test pass criterion (experiment 3)

At 3 of 3 targets: the sag baseline recorded at the raise and at the high waypoint (both lines present, 0.02-0.07 m); the corrected
`pre:` err under 0.006 m; five `airN: moved` lines between 0.007 and 0.012 m; the `slide end:` error under 0.010 m; the owner's
elbow-to-torso and elbow-to-stand gaps at least 5 cm (`o` lines); the retreat and the retraced descent completed without contact.
Fail at any target: no kill test on this plan.

## Risks

- The motion layer has never moved the real arm. The air test is the first motion: half speed (`--speed 0.5`), hand open, no can,
  the path of every move solved before it starts, a 2 cm per-target cap, IK within 5 mm, 5 degree joint-limit margins.
- The return home retraces the recorded raise; without one (an arm left out over the table at start) it is a joint-space move that is
  only reported, not checked, until `table_edge_m` is taped.
- With a 1-1.5 cm error in the measured can axis along the approach (the mock shows it for cans at y >= 0.17 and right of centre: the
  axis-aligned box of a slanted can, as Tuesday's 58 mm Chi measured 70 mm), a grasp approach aborts on contact or closes short: safe,
  but it limits grasp success until the axis comes from the depth points. No grasp is in this plan.
- `V2_REVIEW_findings.json` holds 113 reviewer findings not yet triaged; none of the ones checked so far changes the air test or the
  kill test, but `--palm-real` does not reach the kinematics (bound at import) and must not be overridden on the command line.
