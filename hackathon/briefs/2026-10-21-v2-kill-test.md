# Run plan: v2-kill-test (2026-10-21, the next robot session)

Written by the v2 build session (S0 draft); the owner approves it before the robot is switched on. One experiment: the hand measured
in the head camera at the pre-grasp (V2_STRATEGY experiment 2), preceded by the swivel-and-slide air test (experiment 3) as the first
motion of the session, because v2 moves the arm on a new planner and a new motion layer. No grasps in this plan.

## Question

Does the head camera measure the hand's features (fingertip front, nearest face, height) against the can axis with a scatter under
1 cm, and does one commanded correction leave a residual under 1 cm, at three can positions?

## The deciding number

- Measured quantity, unit, frame: `off` = features - can axis along (forearm d, palm normal n, z), metres, pelvis frame, from the
  `probe:` line (median of 5 frames) and `scatter` (std over the 5 frames).
- Tolerance: scatter < 0.010 m on each axis; after `--probe-servo` with K, the residual |e_d|, |e_z| < 0.010 m.
- Pass criterion with n per class: scatter under 1 cm at 3 of 3 positions (y about 0.15-0.18, 0.10, 0.05-0.07; one can, slim or
  standard, the same can throughout); the measured-minus-model offset (`off` minus `model_off`) changes by 2-4 cm across the
  positions (the configuration-dependent model error the servo is for); the residual under 1 cm within two iterations at 2 of 3.
- Fail means: the blob is not a usable hand feature (scatter >= 1 cm, or the hand not seen at a position the camera should cover):
  the next plan measures with an ArUco tag on the glove before anything else is built (V2_STRATEGY section 5); no servo-based grasp
  is attempted on this code.

## Stop rules for this plan

The general rules in `DEV_WORKFLOW.md` section 4 apply. Plan-specific:

- The dry run's target disagrees with the tape by more than 3 cm, or the plane by more than 2 cm: stop, the camera transform or
  the table fit is wrong; no motion.
- Air test: any contact of the elbow or forearm with the torso, the stand or its straps at a planned psi: L2+B, stop; the bands or
  the thresholds are wrong, no more motion on this plan (`--clear` only after the gap is measured and written).
- Air test: an `up:` / `high:` / `pre:` line with `err` over 0.02 m, or a `SKILL FAILED` IK message: stop; the model disagrees
  with the arm, the kill test is not run.
- Kill test: "hand not seen" at two of three slots, or N under 300 points, or the blob bbox not on the hand in `measure_probe.jpg`:
  stop and read the frames; the ArUco plan follows.
- Kill test: `off` minus `model_off` larger than 8 cm on any axis, or `gemma hand vs blob` over 3 cm at two slots: stop, the camera-to-pelvis transform, the blob or the palm model is wrong.
- Dry run: "I cannot see the table" at a slot: the plane fit failed; read `bundle_locate_depth.png` before any motion (no box-depth fallback exists in v2).
- Two identical faults, or the owner's "stop": the block ends.

## Owner tasks

- Before: tape the three slots and the table height; name and photograph the can; the boot checklist; approve this plan.
- During: L2+B through every motion; at the air-test pre-grasp, the elbow-to-torso and elbow-to-stand gaps by eye (>= 5 cm or not);
  at each kill-test pause, whether the palm is centred on the can along the forearm (`b` behind / `c` centred / `f` forward) and in
  height (`l` low / `o` ok / `h` high), typed as `o fore=c height=o note=...`; reset the can to the tape only with the arm paused (`p`).
- After: which slot looked centred; what the hand did that the numbers do not show; what to measure next time.

## Risks

- The new motion layer streams its own IK; the cross-check against g1-fetch's FK refuses a disagreeing waypoint, the per-target cap is
  2 cm, and the IK tolerance is 5 mm, but the arm has never moved on it: the air test is the first motion, half speed, hand open.
- The park pose (joint zero) puts the real palm about 36 cm ahead, 15 cm left, at table height: the slots must avoid it, or the
  session uses `--park home`.
- The head camera's view of the hand at the far-left slot may be at the image edge (Oct 19: the hand box touched the left edge);
  a "hand not seen" there is expected and not a failure of the method.
