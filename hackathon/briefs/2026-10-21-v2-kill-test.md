# Run plan: v2-kill-test (the next robot session)

Written by the v2 build session (S0 draft, revised after `hackathon/brain/V2_REVIEW.md` rounds 1-3); the owner approves it before the
robot is switched on. Two experiments of V2_STRATEGY, no grasps: the swivel-and-slide air test (experiment 3) as the first motion of the
session, because v2 moves the arm on a new planner and a new motion layer, then the hand measured in the head camera at the pre-grasp
(experiment 2, the kill test). Every brain command carries `--park home --no-grasp --expect-calib ae553548d47b677a --table-z T`, and every
command that may move the arm also `--table-edge E --fingertip F --palm-half-height H` (the owner's tape, below; without E the arm is not
enabled). The agent types the commands and the `o` lines; the owner speaks the judgements and keeps L2+B.

## Question

Does the head camera measure the hand's features (fingertip front, nearest face, height) against the can axis with a scatter under
1 cm, and does one commanded correction leave a residual under 1 cm, at three can positions the camera covers?

## The deciding number

- Measured quantity, unit, frame: `off` = features - can axis along (forearm d, palm normal n, z), metres, pelvis frame, from the
  `probe:` line (median of 5 frames) and `scatter` (std over the 5 frames, printed on the same line and in `orders.jsonl` under `probe`).
- Tolerance: scatter < 0.010 m on each axis; after `--probe-servo` with K and a 2.5 cm pre-grasp offset, the residual |e_d|, |e_z|
  < 0.010 m.
- Pass criterion with n per class: at the three kill-test slots (x 0.38, y 0.12 / 0.09 / 0.06; the purple 12.2 cm can throughout):
  scatter under 1 cm at 3 of 3; the hand in view at 3 of 3 (the `probe:` line shows `margins image A px window B px` with A and B at
  least 8 and none of "AT THE IMAGE EDGE", "AT THE WINDOW EDGE", "NEAR FACE INSIDE THE CAN", "gemma hand box: none"); `measure_probe.jpg`
  shows the blob box on the hand with the fingertips inside it; in the servo block, the residual under 1 cm within two iterations at
  2 of 2 slots. Scatter tests stability, not accuracy: the mock's `off` is not a prediction (a box hand with full depth returns).
- `off` minus `model_off` is recorded at each slot and judged only against wide expected ranges (stop rules): d 0.03 to 0.12, n 0.00 to
  0.04, z 0.00 to 0.07 (the mock: 0.093-0.097, 0.020, 0.043-0.044 at y 0.09).
- Edge probe, reported separately and outside the pass criterion: slot y 0.17. With the URDF camera mount the hand at that pre-grasp
  touches the left image border; expect "AT THE IMAGE EDGE" (then the robot says "Not measured" and no `off` is recorded).
- Fail means: the blob is not a usable hand feature (scatter >= 1 cm, or the hand not seen at a slot the camera covers): the next plan
  measures with an ArUco tag on the glove before anything else is built (V2_STRATEGY section 5); no servo-based grasp on this code.

## Stop rules for this plan

The general rules in `DEV_WORKFLOW.md` section 4 apply. Plan-specific:

- `CODE 13bff01` missing, `(dirty)` on the calib line, or `CALIB IDENTITY FAILED` / `REFUSED: no --expect-calib`: stop, no motion.
- The dry run's target disagrees with the tape by more than 3 cm, or the plane by more than 2 cm: stop, the camera transform or the
  table fit is wrong; no motion. An air target's table check refuses by itself (no table seen under it, or more than 2 cm from T).
- The 0.30 m zone is pelvis x < 0.30 (about 17-22 cm in front of the chest), |y| < 0.12, z 0.15-0.50 above the pelvis (0.99-1.34 m above
  the floor); the head camera sees only up to about 0.36-0.46 of it. The hand held at 10 or 20 cm is not refused, or one at 30 cm is
  refused, or `valid depth` is under 80% or `largest blind region` over 8% with nothing in front: stop; the rule cannot see that zone on
  this robot.
- E: the `dry raise from home` line's X must be at most E - 0.02 and E at most 0.347 (the purple can at x 0.38 stands on the table): with
  the mock's 12.5 cm hand X is 0.304-0.307, so E between 0.33 and 0.347 (chest-to-edge 25-26.7 cm). Outside that window: stop, move the
  stand or the slots. The brain refuses any raise, retreat, home or park path that passes within 2 cm of E below the table top + 3 cm.
- Air test: any contact of the elbow, forearm or hand with the torso, the stand, its straps or the table: L2+B, then `quit!`; no more
  motion on this plan (`--clear` only after the gap is measured and written).
- Air test: a `SKILL FAILED` message (the brain refuses a corrected `pre:` err over 0.02 m, a sag over 0.07 m and a stalled slide step by
  itself), or `My arm stopped and is holding`: stop; the model disagrees with the arm, the kill test is not run. The `up:` and `high:`
  lines are uncorrected: their err is the sag baseline, recorded (Tuesday logged 1.9-2.6 / 2.5-4.7 cm).
- Kill test and servo block: any contact refusal (`SKILL FAILED: pre-grasp: ... (contact?)`, `servo N: ... (contact?)`, `correction
  stopped: ...`) or the hand seen on the table in `cam3_pregrasp.jpg` or `measure_probe.jpg`: the block ends; compare the dry run's plane with
  the tape before anything else. A taped H over 0.05: run with `--palm-half-height H` (the floor rises with it) and note it.
- Kill test: "hand not seen", "AT THE IMAGE EDGE", "AT THE WINDOW EDGE", "NEAR FACE INSIDE THE CAN" or "gemma hand box: none" at two of the
  three covered slots, N under 300 points, or the blob bbox not on the hand in `measure_probe.jpg`: stop and read the frames; the ArUco
  plan follows.
- Kill test: `off` minus `model_off` outside d 0.03-0.12, n 0.00-0.04, z 0.00-0.07, or the Gemma hand box on the blob under 50% at two
  slots: stop, the camera-to-pelvis transform, the blob or the palm model is wrong.
- Dry run: "I cannot see the table" or "at the edge of my view" at a slot: read `bundle_locate_depth.png` and move the slot before any
  motion (no box-depth fallback exists in v2).
- Something in the 0.30 m zone during a move (the brain checks again before high, before the lowering and before every air and approach
  step): the arm holds where it is ("Something is too close in front of me", then the stuck sentence); the person steps back; then the
  stuck procedure.
- **Stuck** (the robot says "My arm stopped and is holding. Press h to see the way out."; a refused or stopped move): the arm holds its
  last target and nothing moves without the operator; the reminder repeats every 30 s. Key by key:
  1. L2+B ready.
  2. `h` + Enter: the brain plans the way out from where the arm is and says it, nothing moves, for example "The way out: retreat back
     17 centimetres, then turn the wrist 70 degrees, then home 38 centimetres. Press y to run it." The distances are straight lines
     between the palm's end points, not the path; "turn the wrist" is the bridge onto the recorded raise.
  3. `y` + Enter within 30 s runs it, announced ("Up and back.", "Turning my wrist.", "Home."); a part already done is not repeated (the
     arm never walks further out). `n`, anything else, or no answer keeps holding.
  4. `a`, `q` or Ctrl-C during the way out stops the arm there: back to step 2. `q` or Ctrl-C while stuck does not release anything:
     the brain quits after the next way out has brought the arm home.
  5. "I have no safe way out", a way out the owner does not like, or anything unexpected: the owner holds the arm, presses L2+B
     (damping), then the agent types `quit!` (or `quit` while stuck): the brain exits without moving or releasing the arm. Never `quit!`
     without L2+B. Leaving damping while the brain still runs brings back the held target, so exit first. Then the owner lowers the arm
     by hand to the side; the next command's start refuses an arm not at the side.
  6. After a crash of the brain with the arm out (a traceback, no prompt): the owner holds the arm and presses L2+B, then lowers it by hand.
- `q` or Ctrl-C with the hand out is not a stop: the brain brings the arm home first ("Moving my arm back before I stop.", about 25 s at
  half speed) and releases it only at home; a second `q`, `a` or Ctrl-C (also two typed at once) stops the arm there: the stuck
  procedure. The only stop is L2+B.
- Can resets: `p` between orders: the robot says "Releasing my arm for the reset." and the console shows `== ARM OFF`; only then the can
  is moved, hands away; `p` again: "Taking my arm." (the start-up checks) and `== ARM ON`; then Enter for the next slot. Enter typed while
  paused is ignored.
- Two identical faults, or the owner's "stop": the block ends.

## Air-test pass criterion (experiment 3)

At 4 of 4 runs: the sag baseline lines at the raise and at the high waypoint present (recorded, no lower bound; over 0.07 m the brain
stops by itself); the corrected `pre:` err under 0.006 m; the five `airN: moved` lines adding up to at least 0.045 m (each step's own
lower bound dropped; the brain stops a step under 0.005 m by itself); the `slide end:` error under 0.010 m; the owner's elbow-to-torso and
elbow-to-stand gaps as the model expects (above); the retreat and the retraced descent completed without contact and without a stuck.
Fail at any run: no kill test on this plan.

## Risks

- The motion layer has never moved the real arm. The air test is the first motion: half speed (`--speed 0.5`: every Cartesian speed,
  the approach steps and the joint step per target halved), hand open, no can, every path solved before it starts, a 2 cm per-target
  palm cap and a 0.01 rad per-target joint cap at half speed, IK within 5 mm, 5 degree joint-limit margins (the raise waypoint leaves
  the elbow 6-11 degrees from its limit in the mock), the keep-out against E with the taped F.
- The raise goes up beside the hip first (2 cm behind it, to the table top + 3 cm), then to the raise waypoint: with a 12.5 cm hand the
  straight raise swept the fingertips to x 0.35 under the table top. The return home retraces the recorded raise from wherever a stop
  left the arm; a joint-space move only from below the table-top height behind the raise waypoint; anywhere else the arm holds (stuck).
- The table floor uses the hand box (F, H, the assumed back extent 0.045 and half thickness 0.02) through the predicted orientation on
  arrival; the kill test's 2 cm margin covers a plane 2 cm low with the hand 1 cm low (mock push 3 mm). A larger plane error: the
  contact refusals.
- The 0.30 m rule refuses under 70% valid depth and on one blind region over 8% with the arm at home; something small inside the D435i's
  minimum range with the arm out may not be seen (the no-motion check with a hand at 10, 20 and 30 cm decides what it sees).
- The hand blob's search (a 200 x 170 px window, 25 cm radius, grown once when touched) can take in an object the detector does not see;
  only detected cans are excluded; the near-face check catches can points in the blob.
- Grasps are not in this plan (`--no-grasp` on every command): the transit height over other cans, the visual gap's dependence on K_n,
  the blind stall-pattern nudge and the can-axis bias are recorded in `hackathon/brain/HANDOFF.md` for the grasp session.
- `V2_REVIEW_findings.json` holds 113 round-1 findings not yet triaged; `--palm-real` does not reach the kinematics (bound at import)
  and must not be overridden on the command line.
